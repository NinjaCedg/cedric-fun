THE FNOL UPRISING

It is a period of transition within the Qover galaxy. Rebel engineers, working from a hidden sprint, have secured a major victory against the Galactic Empire of third-party tools.

 

For too long, the Empire’s rigid forms—Paperform, Jotform, and Penbox—have held the claim intake process hostage, demanding manual work and offering no real-time connection to the core systems.

 

Armed with the blueprints for a new NATIVE BUILDER, the resistance prepares to strike. But the danger is far from over. Imperial remnants still occupy 80% of the platform’s products.

 

As the team prepares for the final push, they seek to restore sovereignty, enable SSO, and bring true whitelabeling to every partner in the star system. Only then will the FNOL be truly free....
